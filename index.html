Kanbanero organises work into boards, columns, and tasks. Add columns for the stages of your workflow: To Do, In Progress, Done, or whatever fits. Drag tasks between them as work moves forward. Click any task to edit it. ⌘N creates a new task instantly.

Columns can be renamed and reordered by dragging. Save any board as a template to reuse across projects. When you have multiple boards, ⌘L brings up the project list so you can switch without losing focus. Appearance is customisable too: light and dark themes, fonts, colour accents, and animated shader backgrounds.

Frequently asked questions

Does Kanbanero work offline?

Yes. Kanbanero is a Mac app that runs entirely on your machine. It never needs an internet connection, never phones home, and never talks to a server.

Where are my boards stored?

In a folder you pick. Every board is a plain .yaml file you can open in any text editor, back up, diff, or commit to Git. Prefer JSON? Pick .json in Settings → Storage.

Can I sync boards between Macs?

Yes. Just put your boards folder in iCloud Drive, Dropbox, Syncthing, or a Git repo. Kanbanero stays out of it: sync is whatever you already use.

Can I use it with Git and pull requests?

Absolutely. Because boards are YAML, you can check them into your project repo, review changes in diffs, and keep task state alongside your code.

Is there a subscription?

No. Kanbanero is a one-time $8.99 purchase on the Mac App Store. No subscriptions, no accounts, no in-app purchases.

Does it work on Apple Silicon and Intel?

Yes, it's a universal binary and requires macOS 12 or later.

Is there an iOS, Windows, or Linux version?

Not today. Kanbanero is macOS-only. Since boards are plain YAML, you can read and edit them from any platform with any text editor.

Does Kanbanero track me or collect analytics?

No. No accounts, no telemetry, no tracking. Your data stays on your disk.
